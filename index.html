About

I am a second year doctoral student interested in the Langlands program. Recently, I have been thinking about objects and techniques arising out of the Geometrization of Local Langlands Conjecture. I have also been thinking about categorical traces and its applications to geometric representation theory.

I obtained by B.S. and M.S. in mathematics from the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru.
